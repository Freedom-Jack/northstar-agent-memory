Hidden sub-tasks go through the vendors' APIs. It is the route a product can build on (plan logins in third-party products need Anthropic's approval), it leaves your chat allowance alone, and the plan-login scripting route may close.

The four things that still run apart, and why

  1. The blind version check, in a fresh context of the same AI. The conversation holds the versions, their order and your praise, so it cannot judge blind; judges favour versions labelled "new" and the one users like. The tests compare it with your assistant checking in its own reply, and the simpler one wins a tie.
  2. Maintenance checks, by the other family. Similar models make similar errors, and a check never runs on the family that did the work or said the claim. You allowed the excerpts this needs.
  3. A trigger for maintenance. Claude's own scheduled tasks (while the Claude app is open, or at its next launch) or the Mac's scheduler. It starts your AI; it is not an AI.
  4. A small search model on this Mac. It finds related items said in other words; it writes and judges nothing. Keyword search alone is the alternative.

The chat apps have no hooks, so there focus works only when you ask. Northstar's own runs are never captured as your sessions.

  • From ChatGPT's Dreaming: a background process, memory kept current, time-bound facts re-dated, and a page to see and correct it. OpenAI does not say how it stores or checks its memory state.
  • From Codex memories (open code): each idle session distilled, then one step that integrates. Codex builds summaries from summaries, which we avoid.
  • From Anthropic's Dreams (preview): a new copy you keep or discard, never changing the old one. Our safety copy is the same idea.
  • Why background work pays: working ahead between sessions used about 5× less compute at answer time, and was up to 13–18% more accurate, when the next request was predictable. Resuming your own work is predictable.

RisksWhat could go wrong, and what we do about it
  1. The moments catch about half of your moments (6 of 13, in-sample). You can always ask; smarter triggers are tested before use.
  2. A wrong suggestion is costly and a false raise feels like nagging, so silence is the default and the check says "can't tell" when unsure.
  3. Focus thinks inside the conversation that drifted, and its note stays in the chat's history. The tests check both, and a hidden sub-task takes over only if it does better.
  4. It costs more than we first said: about $25–85 a month through the APIs. A cap you set, and the first week measured.
  5. Checks across vendors send excerpts between Anthropic and OpenAI: allowed by you, limited to what a check needs, and logged.
  6. AI judges are weakest on close versions and on taste, so the quality check is only a second opinion.
  7. Maintenance can drift over months, as other systems do, so every item cites its source, a safety copy is checked first, and an eight-week drift test runs.
  8. The chat apps have no hooks: there focus works only when you ask. ChatGPT may allow only read access on a personal plan.
  9. The overview's look is unproven until a sketch lands.
